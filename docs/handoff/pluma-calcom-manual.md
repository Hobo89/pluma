## Fields, privacy and policy

- Keep name/email necessary for booking. Audit showed a mandatory WhatsApp number: justify its need or make it optional. Do not require the customer to use WhatsApp if normal telephone contact is all that is needed.
- Remove the broad “share anything” comment prompt, or replace it with optional logistics/preferences only: EN “Practical preferences only; please do not include medical information.” ES “Solo preferencias prácticas; no incluyas datos médicos.”
- Provide access to Pluma's own privacy information at collection. Provider terms alone do not explain Pluma's processing. Coordinate the exact field inventory with Cursor.
- Publish the actual approved cancellation, rescheduling, lateness and no-show rules, including monetary/bono consequences, before confirmation. Do not adopt an arbitrary 24-hour deadline or automatic forfeiture from a template.
- Review connected calendars, permissions, account access/MFA, processing agreement and relevant transfers with the operational/legal checklist. Do not give the website agent account credentials.

## Availability and confirmation

- Verify real working days, buffers, minimum notice, advance window and daily workload. Do not infer these from past drafts. Make different-duration events share the same busy-time checks.
- Confirmation must state the selected duration, final price, local date/time, payment arrangement, approved policy and working cancellation/reschedule links.
- Put accurate private arrival directions, entrance/floor/lift/contact details in the appropriate confirmation. This does not replace the website's legally required business identification.
- Ensure no “call”, video-meeting or online-location language remains for an in-person massage.
- Use only authorised test contacts/events for tests. Confirm actual email delivery; reaching the contact form is not completion. Do not send test messages to real customers.
