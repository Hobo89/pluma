# Locked decisions

## Language and voice

- English locale: American English (`en-US`).
- Spanish locale: Spain Spanish (`es-ES`).
- Stephen is the narrator. Use “I” for his work and “we” only for what Stephen and the client do together.
- The approved English file is the source text. The Spanish file is a fluid localization, not a literal translation.
- Trust comes from concrete details and clear boundaries, not repeated claims such as “professional,” “premium,” or “certified.”

## Vouchers

- Vouchers are available now through a direct inquiry journey.
- English customer term: **voucher**. Spanish customer term: **bono**.
- Available session lengths: 60 or 90 minutes only.
- Available quantities: five or ten sessions.
- Five sessions: 10% discount; valid for three months from the first session.
- Ten sessions: 15% discount; valid for six months from the first session.
- The first session must occur within 30 days after issue.
- The digital voucher is sent by email.
- Primary CTA label: **Ask about a voucher** / **Preguntar por un bono**.
- CTA destination: `https://cal.com/pluma-massage/valencia`.
- No online checkout or payment flow.
- Do not disclose payment methods on the website.
- Do not mention a digital wallet, compatible wallets, phone wallet, pass provider, live balance, wallet testing, or wallet work in progress.
- The authoritative voucher log and balance remain in the internal Notion CRM. Do not expose Notion or describe this internal process to clients.
