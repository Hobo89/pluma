# Pluma — legal and operational launch checks

Prepared 9 September 2026 for Cursor and the owner. Official sources were checked for this brief. This is an implementation checklist, not a legal opinion or approval to operate. Apply it to the actual service and business facts; do not turn unresolved questions into public legal assurances.

## 1. Registration and home-studio operation

Owner states that autónomo registration is not complete. Importass says registration must be requested before starting self-employed activity, and explains coordination with Hacienda. Prepare the website now, but treat confirmed registration/start date as a transactional-launch dependency. Do not present an invented registration status or assume low revenue creates an exemption. Have the gestor resolve the actual start date and any existing activity. [Seguridad Social: alta en trabajo autónomo](https://portal.seg-social.gob.es/wps/portal/importass/importass/Categorias/Altas%2C%2Bbajas%2By%2Bmodificaciones/Altas%2By%2Bafiliacion%2Bde%2Btrabajadores/Alta_trabajo_autonomo).

A home studio's permitted use and opening procedure need a premises-specific answer from València's Servicio de Actividades or a competent technician. Municipal services include activity-opening procedures; that does not prove this studio qualifies for an innocuous-activity procedure. Verify use compatibility, applicable activity process, access/fire requirements and any lease/community restrictions. Do not invent a licence number or assert no licence is needed. [Ajuntament de València: municipal appointment/service routes](https://www.valencia.es/cas/tramites/cita-previa/-/content/cita-previa-2020).

Healthcare-centre authorisation is a separate matter. Generalitat procedure 303 governs installation/functioning of healthcare centres and services. It is not evidence that every wellness massage studio needs that authorisation, or that Pluma has it. Actual activities and professional scope determine relevance. [Generalitat: sanitary authorisation](https://sede.gva.es/es/detall-tramit?id_proc=303).

## 2. Identity and final prices

Prepare an accessible legal notice with verified operator identity, NIF, appropriate full geographic/business address and contact details. Add registration/authorisation/professional details where applicable, without manufacturing them. A neighbourhood landmark is not a substitute for mandatory identification information. Confirm how the lawful business address and private arrival instructions should differ. Display final consumer prices and tax treatment clearly; no surprise surcharge at the calendar/payment step. Website placeholders do not fulfil these disclosure requirements. [LSSI, Article 10](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2002-13758#a10).

AEAT expressly explains that masoterapistas are outside the listed medical/health-professional category for this exemption and their professional services are subject to, and not exempt from, VAT. Do not claim a healthcare VAT exemption simply because the service involves massage or wellbeing. Ask the gestor to confirm classification, rate and invoicing, including prepaid bonos. Keep current displayed prices as draft gross consumer amounts until approved. [AEAT: medical and healthcare exemptions](https://sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es/Sede/ayuda/manuales-videos-folletos/manuales-practicos/manual-iva-2025/capitulo-03-entregas-realizadas-empresarios-profesionales/entregas-bienes-servic-realizadas-empresarios-profesionales/operaciones-exentas/exenciones-operaciones-interiores/exenciones-operaciones-medicas-sanitarias.html).

## 3. Claims, qualifications and imagery

Do not imply physiotherapy, diagnosis, injury rehabilitation, treatment of disease, detoxification, cure or guaranteed therapeutic effects without a verified lawful and evidential basis. “Non-medical” small print does not neutralise contradictory medical promises elsewhere. Review titles, service descriptions, testimonials, image captions, SEO and booking descriptions together. Prefer factual descriptions of touch, pressure, setting and relaxation. Specific pregnancy/lymphatic services need a scope/training review; this brief does not conclude that those names alone are unlawful. Spain regulates advertising of activities/services with purported health purposes. [Real Decreto 1907/1996](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-1996-18085).

Publish training and insurance claims only with evidence. A private course certificate is not automatically a regulated healthcare qualification. Human approval of any new visual must include truthful representation: generated scenes must not purport to show the actual studio, practitioner or customers.

## 4. Sales terms and statutory withdrawal

Before online sales, approve Spanish pre-contract information and matching English terms: identity, total, performance/payment, validity, complaints and applicable withdrawal information. Distinguish ordinary appointment cancellation from statutory withdrawal. The general distance-contract withdrawal period is 14 calendar days, subject to exceptions. An emailed wallet pass does not automatically make an underlying massage package non-refundable digital content. Do not assume the dated leisure-service exception applies. Have the appropriate rule reviewed for single appointments and open-date bonos separately.

If services start during the withdrawal period, implement the applicable express-request/acknowledgement and proportionate-payment rules. Do not insert a blanket waiver. Payment buttons must unambiguously communicate payment obligation; provide durable confirmation. Review expiry/forfeiture terms for fairness and situations where Pluma cannot provide visits. The owner's three/six-month choice is a commercial input, not a legal validation. [Consumer law: Articles 80–87, 97–108](https://www.boe.es/buscar/act.php?id=BOE-A-2007-20555).

Owner must still decide when validity starts, end-of-month handling, extensions, gifting/sharing, duration mixing and remaining-credit treatment. These are unresolved business decisions; the agent must not choose punitive defaults.

## 5. Privacy and health information

GDPR requires transparent information at collection, including controller, purposes/bases, recipients, relevant transfers, retention or criteria, and rights. Review actual services/contracts rather than claiming EU-only processing by default. Minimise booking fields. Health information is special-category data; ordinary booking processing alone is not a sufficient Article 9 condition. Avoid collecting it in a casual comments field, analytics or ordinary pass content. A separate intake process needs its own justified legal basis/condition and safeguards. Keep rights, correction/deletion and processor responsibilities operational, not merely prose. [GDPR: Articles 5, 6, 9, 13, 28 and 32](https://www.boe.es/buscar/doc.php?id=DOUE-L-2016-80807).

Operational email is distinct from marketing. Do not auto-subscribe bookers or bono purchasers to campaigns. Data retention is not the same as bono expiry; determine each purpose's real retention requirements.

## 6. Cookies and third-party services

Audit actual storage/network behaviour. Necessary user-selected language/theme preferences may qualify for exemption; nonessential cookies require appropriate consent. Where needed, offer clearly visible accept and reject options without steering, suitable settings and withdrawal. An absent banner alone does not prove a violation; an installed banner alone does not prove trackers are blocked. Check Cal.com and future payment/wallet dependencies in context. [AEPD: cookie guidance](https://www.aepd.es/guias/guia-cookies.pdf).

## 7. Local complaints process

València provides a procedure for businesses/professionals with establishments open to the public to obtain official Generalitat complaint forms. Confirm and fulfil the requirements for this studio, including any notice obligations, and publish a real complaint contact/process. An email inbox should not be represented as replacing official forms. Do not claim forms are available until they actually are. [València: official complaint forms](https://sede.valencia.es/sede/registro/procedimiento/AE.CO.15?lang=1).
