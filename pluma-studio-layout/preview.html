09 / Working accordion

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this in Cursor?

Copy the system files into your project, add the supplied Cursor rule, import the stylesheet, and paste the implementation brief from CURSOR-PROMPT.md.

Is this the original template code?

No. This is a fresh implementation of the visual patterns observed in your screenshots. Exact fonts, breakpoints, and interactions could not be verified.

Can I use it for Pluma.life?

Yes. Use treatment cards instead of products, licensed massage photography, and your real booking details. Keep the spacing, type hierarchy, and palette.

What needs replacing before launch?

Replace demo content and artwork, supply licensed fonts and images, and connect real booking and form services. Validate the result with your own content.
